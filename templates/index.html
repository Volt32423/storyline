Applied AI final project

Storyline

Helping a grandfather with a fading memory revisit and save his life stories.

How it works

  1. A friendly call. Storyline phones Grandpa {{ grandpa_name }} and gently brings back one memory at a time, never quizzing him.
  2. Old photos. Family members text an old photo, and the next call asks him about it.
  3. A family story book. Every story is saved so the whole family can keep it.

Try it

You'll play Grandpa {{ grandpa_name }}, a made-up grandfather in his 80s whose memory is fading. Enter your number and the demo code from our submission, and you'll get a call within seconds. Calls are capped at 3 minutes.
